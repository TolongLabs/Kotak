## 1 · Who we are · 0:00–0:23 · 48w

Good morning to our dear judges and audience. We are Team T010NG, a team of students from Universiti Melaya and APU.
The product we would like to pitch today is named Kotak, Malay for box, and its goal is to help open the black box surrounding your job.

## 2 · The hook · 0:23–1:28 · 136w

As you may know, throughout the past few years, there has been a general sense of deterioration of the job market.
In July 2026, PERKESO counted this year's retrenchments.
More than half of the employees laid off were professionals, managers, executives and technicians.
People with degrees, in office jobs, who believed they were safe and insulated.
Is AI behind it? Khazanah Research Institute says: not yet, rather than never.
But "not yet" is not a plan. It merely delays the inevitable.
The money to retrain is already there. HRD Corp has allocated 3 billion ringgit for employee training in Budget 2026.
But nobody can tell you which skill to learn first, and which skills can shield you from the horrors of unemployment.
And thus people are left in a state of constant anxiety and learned helplessness.

## 3 · Who it is for · 1:28–2:05 · 77w

True to our team's name, we are here to tolong, which means help in Malay.
Kotak is for people whose job is changing under them right now.
I have a friend that's working as a front-end developer in KL. Let's call her Aina.
Every time a new AI model launches she asks: is my job next on the chopping block?
Her manager has the HRDC funding, but has an unresolved question: which training do we fund first?

## 4 · How it works · 2:05–2:39 · 63w

Kotak works in three steps.
One. Twelve questions about what she actually does at work.
Two. Kotak checks her answers against dated public data on AI use in Malaysian jobs.
Fixed rules decide every result. The AI only explains it.
Three. She gets a plan: the skill to learn first, the hours, and who pays.
Data decides, AI explains. Let's run it live.

## 6 · Why Kotak · 6:10–6:37 · 57w

We compared Kotak with MyMahir, LinkedIn, e-LATiH and a general chatbot.
Kotak is the only one that does five things at once.
A dated AI trend for each skill. The maths shown. A timed plan. A funding route. And a door any AI can call.
Most teams build a chatbot. Kotak is the tool a chatbot calls.

## 7 · Technical architecture · 6:37–7:22 · 94w

Two halves, one rulebook.
The engine runs ahead of time.
Scripts turn dated public data into snapshots, and a weekly check opens a pull request a person approves.
A RunPod GPU wrote the skills and questions for all 923 jobs, and translated ten into Malay and Chinese.
New JobStreet ads arrive daily. Claude sorts them, a person checks them.
The product runs those same rules in your browser. Same answers, same result.
Claude only explains. A verifier throws out any explanation that would change a result.
Any AI assistant can call Kotak's tools directly.

## 8 · What a worker gets · 7:22–8:04 · 87w

What does Aina walk away with?
A check-up that took about three minutes.
A plan that reaches 80% fit by week 34, at ten hours a week.
Every step has a free course, so it costs her nothing.
We ran twenty test profiles. Every one came out with every verdict and milestone carrying a dated source.
Is it fair? We tested thirty pairs differing only in situation, years or qualification.
Verdicts and fit matched in all thirty.
Kotak never asks for name, gender, race, religion or age.

## 9 · Who pays · 8:04–8:33 · 59w

Never the worker.
Employers in HRD Corp's mandatory sectors already pay one percent of wages into a training levy.
Kotak tells them which skills to spend it on. Our next build is a team view for HR.
Agencies get anonymous totals, only for groups of ten or more.
And it's cheap to run. About seven US cents a check-up.

## 10 · The close · 8:33–8:50 · 30w

Kotak means box. We built it to open the box on your job.
We're looking for one HR team to pilot it with their staff.
We're Team T010NG. Thank you.
