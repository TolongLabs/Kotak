# Workshop 1 meeting chat

What the recording of SDC Hackathon 2026 Workshop 1, Alpha Red's "Agentic Development with Cloudflare cf CLI", on 20 October 2026, preserves of the Zoom chat: two messages whose notifications show on screen, and the chat questions KC Cheah answered in the Q&A, with his answers. The workshop's [Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1sWs2gnuY_Mfa_SCvHzEpS1Tw4XYvXYgF) has no chat export, so this is all of the chat that survives. The [transcript](transcript.md) covers what was said, and the [Cloudflare slides](slides-cloudflare.md) and [Orbyte slides](slides-orbyte.md) what was shown.

Until about 00:06:10, Cheong Nan Feng's screen share showed his Chrome window instead of the slides, at times on the tab with the Zoom meeting itself, and Zoom's notification toasts for new chat messages appear in that tab twice. Zoom cuts long names and the recipient short with "...", and the toasts carry no clock time, so messages are timed by the recording (about 10:01 AM at 00:00:00). By the second toast, Zoom's chat button counted 12 unread messages, so most of the chat isn't recorded. In the Q&A, KC answered questions from the chat without reading them out, so those are timed by his answers and their text is lost, except where he restates one.
