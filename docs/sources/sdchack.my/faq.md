## About the event

### What is SDC Hackathon?

SDC: Sustainably Developing Code Hackathon Competition is a hybrid AI hackathon where participants develop AI-based solutions that address real-world challenges aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

### Who is the organizer of SDC Hackathon?

The hackathon is organized by XMUM AI Club, with collaboration from XMUM Rotaract Club and participating university collaborators.

## Eligibility and teams

### Who can participate in SDC Hackathon?

The hackathon is open to foundation, diploma, and undergraduate students from all universities in Malaysia. International students are warmly welcomed as well. Eligibility is based on where you study rather than your nationality, meaning you are eligible as long as you are currently enrolled at a Malaysian university.

Furthermore, the event is open to students from non-technical majors! As long as you have an enthusiasm for coding and a willingness to present your ideas, you are highly encouraged to participate.

### Can students from different universities form one team?

Yes. A team may consist of members from the same university or different universities.

### Can I join as a solo participant?

Yes. Participants may join individually, as the team size is 1 to 5 members per team.

### What is the maximum number of members allowed in one team?

Each team may consist of up to 5 members.

### Can one participant join more than one team?

No. Each participant may only be registered under one team throughout the competition.

### Can we change team members after registration?

No. Team member changes are not allowed after registration has been completed, nor after the event has officially started.

## Registration

### What is the registration fee?

The registration fee is RM40 + RM25 commitment deposit (refundable) per team for Early Bird registration, or RM50 + RM25 commitment deposit (refundable) per team for Normal registration. Early Bird is the first 20 teams to register — not a deadline, so once 20 teams are in, the fee is RM50. The registration page shows how many Early Bird slots are left. The RM25 commitment deposit is returned to your team after the event.

### Where do we register?

Registration must be completed through the official SDC Hackathon website. Read the [terms and conditions](terms-and-conditions.md) first.

## Communication

### Is joining the Discord server compulsory?

Yes. All team members are required to join the official Discord server because important announcements, updates, schedule changes, and instructions will be shared there.

### What happens if I miss an announcement on Discord?

Participants are responsible for checking Discord regularly. Failure to read official announcements will not be accepted as a valid reason for missing deadlines, instructions, or required activities.

### Where can we ask formal questions?

Participants may contact the organizing committee through the official event email for formal inquiries.

- [Official Discord server](https://discord.gg/p72amSbw7X)
- Official email: [sdc.xmumhack@gmail.com](mailto:sdc.xmumhack@gmail.com)

## Problem statement

### When will the problem statement be revealed?

The official problem statement will be revealed online on 19 October 2026, 08:00:00 PM MYT (UTC+8).

### Where will the problem statement reveal take place?

The problem statement reveal will be conducted online. The access link and related instructions will be shared through the official Discord server.

### Are participants required to attend the problem statement reveal?

Yes. Participants are required to attend the online announcement and review the challenge details before starting the competition.

## Judging and submission

### How many teams will be shortlisted for the Final Phase?

Up to 15 teams per domain are expected to be shortlisted for the Final Phase, subject to confirmation by the organizing committee.

### Can we change our submission after submitting?

Submission changes after the deadline will not be accepted unless otherwise announced by the organizing committee.

### What happens if we submit late?

Late submissions may not be accepted and may affect the team's eligibility for judging.

### What should we do if our submission link is not working?

Teams are responsible for ensuring that all links, files, repositories, and submitted materials are accessible before the deadline. The organizing committee may not be responsible for issues caused by incorrect, broken, or inaccessible submissions.

## Physical Final Phase logistics

### What should participants bring for the physical Final Phase?

Participants should bring their own:

- Laptop and any necessary devices
- Charger
- Extension cord
- Sleeping bag or blanket
- Jacket
- Toiletries including towels
- Clothes
- Personal items
- Any tools required for development and presentation

### Are participants responsible for their own belongings?

Yes. Participants are responsible for keeping their personal belongings, devices, and valuables safe at all times.

### Will Wi-Fi be provided?

Wi-Fi access is expected to be provided during the physical Final Phase. Participants should also ensure that their own devices are ready before the event.

### Will accommodation or resting areas be provided during the physical phase?

Yes. Resting areas will be provided during the physical phase of the hackathon. Participants may use the assigned resting areas according to the instructions given by the organizing committee.

However, participants are responsible for bringing their own personal items, such as sleeping bags, jackets, toiletries, extension plugs, chargers, and any other items they may need during the event. These personal items will not be provided by the organizing committee.

### Will shower facilities be available?

Yes. Shower facilities will be available for participants during the physical phase. Participants are advised to bring their own toiletries, towel, change of clothes, and other personal hygiene items.

### Will transportation be provided?

No. Transportation will not be provided. Participants are responsible for arranging and covering their own travel to and from Xiamen University Malaysia.

The organizing committee may provide general directions or venue guidance to help participants locate the event areas on campus.

### Is there a dress code for the event?

Participants are encouraged to wear comfortable and appropriate attire during the hacking sessions. For the pitching session and closing ceremony, participants are advised to wear smart casual or formal attire to maintain a professional presentation standard.

## Mentorship, data and tech stack

### Will mentors be available to guide participants?

Yes. Mentors will be available during selected sessions to provide guidance, feedback, and advice to participants, including on problem understanding, solution direction, technical development, presentation preparation, and overall project improvement.

However, mentors are not allowed to directly build, code, design, or complete the project on behalf of any team.

### Will datasets be provided?

No fixed dataset will be provided unless otherwise stated in the official domain or problem statement guidelines. Participants may use suitable public datasets, self-collected data, open-source resources, or other permitted data sources, as long as they comply with the competition rules and properly acknowledge the source where required.

Participants are advised to refer to the official domain-specific folder or guideline for the latest updates regarding dataset requirements, restrictions, or recommended resources.

### Is there any specific programming language required?

No specific programming language is required. Participants may use any programming language, framework, library, or development tool that they are comfortable with, as long as it is suitable for building their proposed solution.

Participants are encouraged to use technologies that help them develop a functional and presentable prototype or minimum viable product. Teams should also be prepared to explain their technical choices, implementation process, and how their solution works during submission or pitching.
