## Team page

The [team page](https://sdchack.my/portal), where signing in lands.

### Competition documents

Now available. Everything for the preliminary round is in one Google Drive folder: the problem statements for both domains and every document your team needs to read. Read it before you start building. The folder is cleaned into Markdown under [Participant Drive sources](/docs/sources/drive.google.com/README.md).

### Registration status

"Payment verified. You're fully registered — nothing else is needed. We'll email you before Orientation Day with what happens next."

Our team, T010NG, registered on 8 October 2026 with 5 members and paid RM 75 in total: RM 50 registration + RM 25 commitment fee, refunded to your team after the competition ends. The fee was set when the team registered and doesn't change. The page also shows who paid and the account the commitment fee is refunded to.

### Members and Discord access

The page lists each member with their email and university, and marks the team leader.

Discord access: "Give each member their own link. They'll choose Discord themselves; links are private and expire after four days." Each member's row shows their Discord username once connected, with a **New link** button, or **Not connected** with a **Copy link** button.

Members can't be added or removed after registering. Something spelled wrong? Email [sdc.xmumhack@gmail.com](mailto:sdc.xmumhack@gmail.com) and we'll correct it.

### WhatsApp community

A second platform for announcements to all participants. Please join the community so you don't miss any important updates. The **Join the WhatsApp community** button holds the invite link.

### RunPod vouchers

One unique RunPod voucher per participant — never shared. Copy yours from the list below: this page is the durable copy, so every voucher your team was emailed stays here even if an email never arrives.

At capture, our team leader had a voucher and the other four members' entries read "Your voucher is on the way — the committee is getting more from RunPod."

### Mentorship session

Booking is open until 25 October 2026. Each team books one session — times are limited and first come, first served, so we recommend booking early. The **Book a mentor** button opens [mentor booking](#mentor-booking).

### Your project submission

## Submission page

The [submission page](https://sdchack.my/submit) shows the team's domain, Domain 1 - AIVERSECITY. "The full problem statement is in the competition documents folder at the top of your team page (/portal) — this page is only for the GitHub link."

- A checkbox: "Our project integrates our API Premium sponsor's technology — mandatory for both rounds. Submissions without it are disqualified."

You can resubmit as many times as you like until 25 October 2026, 11:59 PM (MYT).

## Mentor booking

The [mentor booking page](https://sdchack.my/mentorship):

Pick a mentor and a time below. This page updates live, so a time another team takes disappears. Each team books one session, and can move or cancel it until 24 hours before it starts. All times are Malaysia time (MYT). Booking closes on 25 October 2026.

Times are limited and given out first come, first served. The committee is working to have a time for every team, but we can't guarantee it — so we recommend booking as early as you can.

Each time is marked open, yours, taken or not bookable yet.
