## 1. Introduction

### 1.1. Acceptance of terms

1.1.1. By registering for or participating in the SDC Hackathon 2026, all participants agree to comply with and be legally bound by these Terms and Conditions.

1.1.2. These Terms and Conditions govern every phase of the Competition, including but not limited to registration, participation, project development, submission, judging, the physical final round, prize distribution, and all related activities organised by the Organiser.

1.1.3. Participants are responsible for reading and understanding these Terms and Conditions before submitting their registration. Failure to comply with any provision contained herein may result in penalties, including but not limited to mark deductions, disqualification, forfeiture of prizes, or other actions deemed appropriate by the Organiser.

1.1.4. By completing the registration process, participants acknowledge that they have read, understood, and accepted these Terms and Conditions in their entirety.

### 1.2. About SDC Hackathon 2026

1.2.1. SDC Hackathon 2026 is a hackathon organised by the AI Club of Xiamen University Malaysia (XMUM), in collaboration with the XMUM Rotaract Club and supporting partners.

1.2.2. The Competition aims to provide university students with opportunities to develop innovative Artificial Intelligence (AI)-driven solutions that address real-world challenges aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

1.2.3. The Competition adopts a "Learn-Then-Build" format consisting of an online preliminary phase, an online finalist upgrading phase, and a physical grand final where shortlisted teams further refine and present their solutions before an independent judging panel.

1.2.4. The Competition may include workshops, mentoring sessions, networking activities, demonstrations, pitching sessions, and other activities determined by the Organiser.

### 1.3. Definitions

For the purposes of these Terms and Conditions, unless the context otherwise requires:

1.3.1. Organiser refers to the SDC Hackathon 2026 Organising Committee.

1.3.2. Participant refers to any individual who has successfully registered for the Competition.

1.3.3. Team refers to a group of one (1) to five (5) registered Participants.

1.3.4. Team Leader refers to the representative officially designated by the Team during registration.

1.3.5. Submission refers to all required deliverables submitted for evaluation, including source code, documentation, presentation materials, GitHub repository, demonstration videos, and any additional materials required by the Organiser.

1.3.6. Finalist refers to any Team shortlisted to participate in the Physical Grand Final.

1.3.7. Sponsor Resources refer to APIs, cloud credits, software licences, development platforms, datasets, or any other resources provided by the Competition sponsors.

1.3.8. Competition Domain refers to each official challenge category or track announced by the Organiser. Each Team shall participate in only one (1) Competition Domain, and Finalists shall be selected independently within each Domain.

### 1.4. Competition objectives

The objectives of SDC Hackathon 2026 include, but are not limited to:

#### 1.4.1. To bridge the gap between theory and application

To provide a structured environment where students can translate academic knowledge into practical, deployed applications through intensive workshops and mentorship.

#### 1.4.2. To solve real-world challenges via AI

The event features two to three specific domains focused on developing AI solutions to any problem that aligns with any SDG ranging from 1 - 17.

#### 1.4.3. To foster inter-university & industry collaboration

To connect the top tech talent from major universities with industry mentors and sponsoring companies, creating a "Tech Festival" atmosphere promoting networking and future employment opportunities.

#### 1.4.4. To cultivate student ingenuity and complex problem-solving

To empower students to apply their ingenuity, creativity, and problem-solving capabilities to meaningful, real-world challenges. By moving beyond traditional case studies, participants are required to deploy design-thinking methodologies and critical analysis to ensure their solutions are not only technologically viable but also socially or economically impactful.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1. Eligible participant

2.1.1. Participation is open to students who are officially enrolled in any degree at any recognised Malaysian higher education institutions.

2.1.2. Eligible participants include students currently pursuing:

- a. Foundation programmes;
- b. Diploma programmes; or
- c. Bachelor's Degree programmes.

2.1.3. Participants must remain enrolled as students throughout the duration of the Competition.

2.1.4. Participants may be required to provide valid student identification or other supporting documents for verification purposes.

2.1.5. The Organiser reserves the right to reject any registration that fails the eligibility verification process.

### 2.2. Ineligible participant

The following individuals are not eligible to participate:

2.2.1. Full-time or part-time working professionals who are no longer enrolled as eligible students.

2.2.1. Individuals who provide false, misleading, or fraudulent registration information.

2.2.2. Committees and any other parties directly involved in organizing, planning, managing, judging, mentoring, or administering the event are not allowed to participate as contestants.

2.2.3. Individuals who fail to satisfy any eligibility requirement specified by the Organiser.

### 2.3. Minor participants

2.3.1. Participants below the age of eighteen (18) years must obtain written consent from their parent or legal guardian before participating in any physical component of the Competition.

2.3.2. The Organiser reserves the right to request additional documentation from minor participants where necessary.

### 2.4. Team eligibility

2.4.1. Each Team shall consist of a minimum of one (1) and a maximum of five (5) Participants.

2.4.2. Teams may comprise participants from different universities or academic institutions.

2.4.3. Every Team must appoint one (1) Team Leader during registration.

2.4.4. The Team Leader shall act as the official representative and primary point of contact between the Team and the Organiser.

2.4.5. All official announcements, notices, requests, finalist confirmations, and prize-related communications shall be sent only to the Team Leader unless otherwise stated.

2.4.6. The Team Leader is responsible for communicating all official information received from the Organiser to every member of the Team.

2.4.7. Each Participant may only belong to one (1) Team throughout the Competition.

2.4.8. A Participant shall not register under multiple Teams or submit multiple entries.

2.4.9. Any violation of this provision may result in immediate disqualification of all affected Teams.

## 4. Competition structure

### 4.1. Competition stages

4.1.1. SDC Hackathon 2026 consists of multiple stages conducted over both online and physical formats.

4.1.2. The Competition schedule shall be announced through the official communication channels.

4.1.3. The Organiser reserves the right to modify the Competition schedule where necessary.

### 4.2. Phase 1: Online preliminary round

4.2.1. The Competition officially commences with the release of the problem statements during the Online Orientation.

4.2.2. Teams shall begin developing their solutions only after the official commencement of the Competition.

4.2.3. During this phase, participants may attend workshops, mentoring sessions, and sponsor activities organised by the Organiser.

4.2.4. Teams shall submit all required deliverables before the specified submission deadline.

### 4.3. Preliminary judging

4.3.1. All eligible submissions shall undergo evaluation by the appointed judging panel.

4.3.2. Only Teams meeting all submission requirements shall be considered for finalist selection.

4.3.3. The judging panel may reject incomplete, invalid, or non-compliant submissions without further review.

### 4.4. Finalist selection

4.4.1. Upon completion of the Preliminary Judging, the top fifteen (15) Teams from each Competition Domain shall be selected as Finalists for the Physical Grand Final, subject to compliance with these Terms and Conditions.

4.4.2. Finalist selection shall be based on the Teams' performance in the Preliminary Judging in accordance with the official judging criteria.

4.4.3. The Organiser reserves the right to select fewer than fifteen (15) Finalists for a Competition Domain if an insufficient number of submissions meet the minimum standard of quality, eligibility, or compliance with these Terms and Conditions.

4.4.4. Finalist Teams shall be officially announced through the Organiser's designated communication channels.

4.4.5. Finalist Teams shall confirm their participation within the deadline specified by the Organiser.

4.4.6. Failure to confirm participation within the stipulated deadline may result in forfeiture of the finalist position, and the Organiser reserves the right to invite the next highest-ranked eligible Team within the same Competition Domain as a replacement.

### 4.5. Phase 2: Online upgrade phase

4.5.1. During this phase, finalists may continue refining their prototypes, documentation, and presentation materials.

4.5.2. Additional mentoring sessions, sponsor consultations, or technical support may be provided at the Organiser's discretion.

### 4.6. Physical grand final

4.6.1. Selected finalists shall participate in the Physical Grand Final held at Xiamen University Malaysia.

4.6.2. Finalists are required to comply with all venue regulations, safety requirements, and event procedures throughout the physical event.

4.6.3. Attendance at the Physical Grand Final is mandatory for all finalist Teams unless otherwise approved by the Organiser.

### 4.7. Pitching session

4.7.1. Each finalist Team shall present its solution during the final pitching session.

4.7.2. The Organiser shall determine the presentation duration, question-and-answer session, presentation format, and pitching order.

4.7.3. Teams that exceed the allocated presentation time may be subject to penalties determined by the Organiser.

4.7.4. Winners of SDC Hackathon 2026 shall be determined based on the final judging results following the pitching session.

## 5. Project development rules

### 5.1. General principles

5.1.1. All Teams are expected to uphold the principles of honesty, fairness, originality, professionalism, and academic integrity throughout the Competition.

5.1.2. Every project submitted to SDC Hackathon 2026 shall be substantially developed during the official Competition period and shall comply with all rules stated in these Terms and Conditions.

5.1.3. Participants are responsible for ensuring that their projects do not infringe upon the intellectual property rights, copyrights, patents, trademarks, trade secrets, or other proprietary rights of any third party.

5.1.4. The Organiser reserves the right to request clarification, supporting evidence, development records, commit histories, or any additional information deemed necessary to verify compliance with these Terms and Conditions.

### 5.2. Originality requirement

5.2.1. Every submission must represent an original work developed by the registered Team.

5.2.2. Projects that are found to be copied, substantially similar to existing works, or intentionally reproduced from publicly available sources without significant original contribution may be disqualified.

5.2.3. The Organiser reserves the right to conduct originality checks using plagiarism detection tools, repository comparisons, internet searches, code similarity analysis, or any other appropriate verification methods.

### 5.3. Pre-existing projects

5.3.1. Projects that were substantially completed before the official commencement of SDC Hackathon 2026 are not eligible for submission.

5.3.2. Teams shall not submit projects that have previously been:

- a. submitted to another hackathon;
- b. entered into another competition;
- c. commercially deployed;
- d. publicly launched; or
- e. previously judged in any similar event.

5.3.3. The Competition officially begins upon the release of the problem statements by the Organiser.

5.3.4. The Organiser may inspect GitHub repository creation dates, commit histories, timestamps, documentation history, and other available records to determine whether a project was substantially developed before the Competition period.

5.3.5. Where sufficient evidence indicates that a project existed prior to the Competition in a substantially similar form, the Organiser reserves the right to disqualify the Team.

### 5.4. Pre-existing code

5.4.1. The use of reasonable amounts of pre-existing code is permitted where such code serves only as a supporting component and does not constitute the primary innovation of the submission.

Examples include but are not limited to:

- a. authentication modules;
- b. reusable utility functions;
- c. database connection utilities;
- d. UI components;
- e. logging systems;
- f. configuration files.

5.4.2. Teams shall not reuse an existing project by making only minor cosmetic modifications.

5.4.3. The core idea, implementation, problem-solving approach, and major functionalities must be developed specifically for SDC Hackathon 2026.

5.4.4. Where requested, Teams must clearly declare any significant pre-existing code incorporated into their project.

5.4.5. Failure to disclose significant pre-existing code may be treated as misconduct.

### 5.5. GitHub repository requirements

5.5.1. Every Team shall develop and maintain its project using an official GitHub repository.

5.5.2. The repository shall be created only after the official commencement of the Competition.

5.5.3. Teams shall not recycle repositories that were created before the Competition unless prior written approval is obtained from the Organiser.

5.5.4. Each Team shall maintain a clear and traceable commit history throughout the Competition.

5.5.5. Git commits should accurately reflect the actual development progress of the Team.

5.5.7. Once the official submission deadline has passed, Teams shall not modify the submitted repository unless expressly instructed by the Organiser.

5.5.8. The Organiser reserves the right to archive or clone repositories for judging and verification purposes.

### 5.6. Use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools

5.6.1. The use of Artificial Intelligence tools is permitted and encouraged during the Competition.

5.6.2. AI tools shall be regarded as productivity assistants rather than substitutes for genuine participant contribution.

5.6.3. Teams remain fully responsible for:

- a. all generated code;
- b. technical correctness;
- c. originality;
- d. licensing compliance;
- e. security vulnerabilities;
- f. ethical use.

5.6.4. The use of AI shall not exempt Teams from complying with plagiarism, originality, intellectual property, or copyright requirements.

5.6.5. The Organiser reserves the right to investigate submissions suspected of being excessively or improperly generated using AI.

### 5.7. Open-source software and libraries

5.7.1. Teams may use publicly available open-source libraries, SDKs, frameworks, and development tools.

5.7.2. All software licences must be respected.

5.7.3. Participants shall comply with the licence terms applicable to every third-party component incorporated into their submission.

5.7.4. Teams are encouraged to acknowledge significant third-party libraries used in their documentation.

5.7.5. The Organiser shall not be responsible for any licensing disputes arising from a Team's use of third-party software.

### 5.8. Frameworks and templates

5.8.1. The use of publicly available development frameworks and starter templates is permitted.

5.8.2. Templates shall only serve as the foundation of development.

5.8.3. The judging panel shall evaluate the originality and technical contribution of the Team rather than the template itself.

5.8.4. Excessive reliance on templates with minimal original development may negatively affect judging.

### 5.9. Sponsor resources

5.9.1. Sponsors may provide participants with complimentary resources.

5.9.2. Such resources shall only be used in accordance with the terms specified by the respective sponsors.

5.9.3. The availability, duration, and conditions of sponsor resources are subject to change without prior notice.

5.9.4. The Organiser does not guarantee the continued availability or functionality of any sponsor-provided resource.

### 5.10. External assistance

5.10.1 All significant development work shall be performed exclusively by the registered Team members.

5.10.2. External parties shall not:

- a. write source code for the Team;
- b. design substantial portions of the solution;
- c. produce deliverables on behalf of the Team;
- d. prepare presentation materials for the Team; or
- e. otherwise perform work that materially contributes to the submission.

5.10.3. Purchasing software development services or outsourcing project development to any third party is strictly prohibited.

5.10.4. The Organiser reserves the right to investigate suspected cases of excessive external assistance.

### 5.11. Ethical development

5.11.1. Teams shall ensure that their projects comply with applicable laws and regulations.

5.11.2. Projects shall not contain content that:

- a. promotes violence;
- b. promotes discrimination or hatred;
- c. facilitates illegal activities;
- d. violates personal privacy;
- e. infringes intellectual property rights;
- f. distributes malicious software;
- g. intentionally compromises cybersecurity; or
