## Redeeming the subscription

1.  Navigate to the Replit dashboard and click **Upgrade your plan**.
1.  Select the **Core** plan.
1.  On the checkout page, locate the promo code field.
1.  Enter the promo code from the team portal and click Apply. Ensure the total due becomes $0.00.
1.  Complete the checkout process using your preferred payment method.

## Setting limits

Marked "IMPORTANT" in the guide: disabling auto-reload.

1.  Immediately after subscribing, you will be taken through an onboarding sequence.
1.  When you reach the **Auto-reload** screen, toggle the switch to disable auto-reload. The guide calls this crucial to prevent automatic top-ups and unexpected charges if you exceed your plan's limits.
1.  Click Continue.

## Cancelling auto-renewal

1.  From your Replit dashboard, click the **Settings** gear icon.
1.  Navigate to the **Billing** tab on the left sidebar.
1.  Scroll down to the bottom and click **Cancel subscription**.
1.  Click **Continue to cancel**, select a reason for leaving, and confirm by clicking **Cancel subscription** again.
1.  You will still have access to the Core plan until the end of your billing cycle.
