# Finalist Drive tour

What the organisers' [SDC Finalist Drive Tour][tour] video says and shows. It's a 3:33 narrated walk through the Finalist Drive, added on 28 October 2026, ending with the campus routes the four Directory videos follow. The narration is merged from two machine transcriptions, OpenAI gpt-transcribe and whisper-1, checked against each other and the screen, with names spelled as in the Drive files.

## What it shows

The title card reads "Finalists · 31 Oct – 1 Nov 2026", "Finalist Drive Tour: Everything for the final." and "Guides, templates, credits and campus directions for finalists." Each step then opens a Drive folder with a numbered label and a short checklist of what matters in it. The Directory steps switch to satellite maps of the campus with the route drawn on, and the virtual-tour step shows a QR code captioned "Scan to explore the campus" and `vt.xmu.edu.my`. The end card reads "SDC Hackathon 2026: Build AI that matters. See you there!"

The recording shows "5. Finalist Credits" holding a subfolder, "3. Sponsor Credits Guidelines", with three PDFs dated 26 October: "1. SDC Hackathon: Finalist Credits Guide (3-in-1)", "2. Replit: Core Subscription Guide (Detailed)" and "3. KrackedDevs: AI Key Guide (Detailed)". When the folder was captured on 28 October, only the [3-in-1 guide](finalist-credits-guide.md) was there, directly inside "5. Finalist Credits", both signed in and signed out.

## Transcript

**[0:00]** Hi finalists, and congratulations. Here's a quick tour of the SDC Hackathon 2026 Finalist Drive so you know where to find everything for the final. Everything is in one shared folder, SDC Hackathon - Finalist Drive. Inside there are six folders: Domain 1, Domain 2, the official handbooks, finalist guidelines, finalist credits, and the directory.

**[0:27]** Let's start with Domain 1. Inside is the [Domain 1 finalist guide](domain-1-aiversecity-finalist-guide.md). Please read it carefully, because the final is different from the preliminary round. Your complete product is judged live, end to end, and all six must-haves must work live. A missing must-have caps your Problem-Solution Fit score. There's a new rubric with six criteria, and your preliminary score doesn't carry forward. For Aiversecity, judges may change your trend data during the demo, check that your courses are real, and ask you to compare at least two future scenarios.

**[1:01]** Domain 2 has the same final standard, in its own [finalist guide](domain-2-orbyte-finalist-guide.md). For Alpha Red, judges want one SME task running live: trigger, data, decision, human approval, and the outcome in the activity log. Approval must really block the action, and you should show how a second business could use your product through Orbyte.

**[1:21]** The official handbooks haven't changed: the official handbook, the terms and conditions, and the participant handbook.

**[1:28]** Next, finalist guidelines, with two subfolders. The GitHub sample folder has a PDF that links to our [sample repository](github-submission-template.md). It's only a reference. Don't start a new repository; keep improving the one from your preliminary round. Template for document submission has the finalist versions of the [submission template](final-report-template.md) and the [technical tools declaration](technical-tools-declaration-template.md#finalist-copy). Please submit new copies of both for the final, because the final asks for additional information.

**[1:58]** Finalist credits has the sponsor credits guidelines. Inside are three guides on how to get your credits as a finalist: the 3-in-1 finalist credits guide, the Replit Core subscription guide, and the KrackedDevs AI key guide.

**[2:12]** Finally, the directory, with four short walkthrough videos to help you find your way around campus. Here's where they go. From the main entrance, the southern gate, follow the road along the field and up the curve to the A3 basement car park. For more details, please watch the video, Main Entrance to A3 Parking Lot.

**[2:33]** From the second entrance, the eastern gate, follow the road south along the eastern side of campus, then around the bottom of the football field, and up to the same car park. For more details, please watch the video, Second Entrance to Parking Lot.

**[2:48]** The shower rooms are in B1. From A3, walk east along the road below the lake, then up to the shower rooms on the east side of B1. For more details, please watch the video, A3 Fifth Floor to Shower Room.

**[3:02]** The prayer room is inside A3 itself, on the fifth floor, so there's no route to show. Just watch the video, A3 Fifth Floor to Prayer Room.

**[3:11]** You can also explore the whole campus with the XMUM virtual tour. Scan the QR code to open it.

**[3:19]** That's the Finalist Drive. Come back here whenever you need a document, a credit guide, or directions. See you at the final.
