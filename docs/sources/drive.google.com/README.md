## Conflicts between sources

The organisers' documents disagree on these points. Check with the organisers, in `#ask-the-organizers` on Discord or at sdc.xmumhack@gmail.com, before relying on the looser rule.

- **Submission deadline**: 25 October, 7:59 AM MYT in the [opening briefing slides](/docs/sources/opening-briefing/slides.md) (slide 47) and the participant handbook, but the Summary Submission video says "You can update it until 25 Oct, 23:59 (MYT)". The earlier time is the safe one.
- **When to create the repository**: before the hacking period begins (participant handbook 6.1.2), but only after the competition officially starts (Terms and Conditions 5.5.2) and "after tonight's problem statement release" (opening briefing, 00:51:46).
- **Repository visibility**: private during the hacking period (participant handbook 6.1.2), but public by submission (participant handbook 10.3, and the submit page checks it). The briefing reconciles them: private while building, public before you submit.
- **Mentor booking**: through the Discord channel (participant handbook 11.2.2), but through the website (official handbook 10.2.2), where both submission videos show it.
- **Formal questions**: the official Discord server (participant handbook 15.4), but the official event email (official handbook 12.13 and the FAQ). Slide 51 of the briefing gives both.
- **Score reviews**: requests are not entertained unless the committee verifies an administrative or technical error (participant handbook 6.8.2), but no appeal or re-evaluation request is entertained at all (Terms and Conditions 7.4.2).
- **Final pitching session**: 6:00 to 8:30 PM on 1 November in A3#517 and A3#516 (participant handbook 6.7.1 and 6.7.3), but 9:40 AM to 3:40 PM in Room 1, A4#113, and Room 2, A5#119 (participant handbook 7.2 and 5).
