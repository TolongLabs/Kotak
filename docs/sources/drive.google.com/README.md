## Conflicts between sources

The organisers' documents disagree on these points. Check with the organisers, in `#ask-the-organizers` on Discord or at sdc.xmumhack@gmail.com, before relying on the looser rule.

The [23 October revisions](/docs/sources/discord.com/announcements.md#document-updates) settled three earlier conflicts. The repository is created after the hacking period begins (participant handbook 6.1.2), mentors are booked through the website (11.2.2), and final pitching follows the 7.2 rundown in A4#113 and A5#119 (6.7.1 and 6.7.3).

- **Submission deadline**: 25 October, 7:59 AM MYT in the [opening briefing slides](/docs/sources/opening-briefing/slides.md) (slide 47) and the participant handbook, but the Summary Submission video says "You can update it until 25 Oct, 23:59 (MYT)", and the website and the [team portal's submission page](/docs/sources/sdchack.my/team-portal.md#submission-page) say 25 October 2026, 11:59 PM (MYT). The earlier time is the safe one. In practice the round closed at 8 AM: the [Discord countdown](/docs/sources/discord.com/announcements.md#submission-countdown) ran to 8 AM, and the closure was announced at 8:07 AM.
- **Repository visibility**: private during the hacking period (participant handbook 6.1.2), but public by submission (participant handbook 10.3, and the submit page checks it). The briefing reconciles them: private while building, public before you submit.
- **Formal questions**: the official Discord server (participant handbook 15.4), but the official event email (official handbook 12.13 and the FAQ). Slide 51 of the briefing gives both.
- **Score reviews**: requests are not entertained unless the committee verifies an administrative or technical error (participant handbook 6.8.2), but no appeal or re-evaluation request is entertained at all (Terms and Conditions 7.4.2).
- **Final pitch deck format**: `.pptx` (participant handbook 6.6.4.6), but "exported and saved as a flattened PDF file" ([final report template](final-report-template.md#6-final-submission-checklist), section 6).
- **Length of the final sprint**: "the 48-hour physical sprint" ([final report template](final-report-template.md#32-implementation--hackathon-deliverables), 3.2), but the participant handbook runs it from 9:30 AM on 31 October to 7:59:59 AM on 1 November (6.6.1 and 6.6.3), under 23 hours, and the [website](/docs/sources/sdchack.my/overview.md#competition-timeline) says top teams "hack for 24 hours".
- **Final deliverables**: the portal's [finalist submission](/docs/sources/sdchack.my/team-portal.md#finalist-submission) takes only a public GitHub repository link, due 1 November, 8:00 AM. The [final report template](final-report-template.md#cover-and-submission-rules) asks for a PDF report together with the pitch deck and a deployment link, and the participant handbook (6.6.4) lists seven deliverables: an updated PRD, an updated tools declaration, a final solution report, a backup pitch video, the repository, `.pptx` slides and live demo materials. Neither says where the files go; in the preliminary round, the [GitHub submission template](github-submission-template.md) kept them in the repository's `submission/` folder.
- **Final pitch and Q&A length**: 10 minutes and 5 minutes (participant handbook 6.7.3), but both finalist guides say the time limit "will be confirmed by the organizers".
- **RunPod vouchers**: "One unique RunPod voucher per participant" ([team portal](/docs/sources/sdchack.my/team-portal.md#runpod-vouchers)), but "Strictly ONE (1) voucher per team, regardless of team size" ([Discord, 22 October](/docs/sources/discord.com/announcements.md#runpod-vouchers)), and the [finalist credits guide](finalist-credits-guide.md#2-runpod) gives US$25 per team.
- **Offline mentoring hours**: the portal's [final round page](/docs/sources/sdchack.my/team-portal.md#offline-mentoring-session) offers slots until 6:50 PM on 31 October, but the participant handbook's 7.2 rundown ends hacking and mentorship at 6:30 PM for the dinner and prayer break.
