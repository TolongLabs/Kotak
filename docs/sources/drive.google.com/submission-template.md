## Cover page and submission rules

The cover page is titled "SDC Hackathon" with the subtitle "Phase 1 : Preliminary Project Proposal", and reads "Hosted by Xiamen University Malaysia (XMUM) AI Club" and "Submission Deadline: 25 October 2026". It carries the SDC logo, a strip of logos (Xiamen University, XMUM AI Club, Rotaract Club of Xiamen University Malaysia, Rotary Club of Kuala Lumpur Dynamics, Rotary Club of Tun Razak Exchange), and a footer with "Instagram: @sdc_xmumhack" and "Email: sdc.xmumhack@gmail.com".

The instructions box on the cover says, verbatim:

> Instructions: Complete every field below and submit as a single PDF document named “SDC2026_TeamName_Preliminary.pdf” before the deadline above. Answers must comply with the word limits stated for each open-ended question. Incomplete submissions may not be eligible for review.

What that means in practice:

- **File**: one PDF named `SDC2026_TeamName_Preliminary.pdf`, with your team name in place of `TeamName`.
- **Deadline**: 25 October 2026. The template gives no time of day.
- **Completeness**: fill in every field. Incomplete submissions may not be eligible for review.
- **Word limits**: 250 words for 2.1, 300 words for 2.2 and 200 words for 3.2. No limit is stated for 3.1, section 4, 6.1, 6.2 or 6.3.
- **Formatting**: the template sets no page limit, font or other formatting rule beyond the single PDF and its file name. Section 3.1 allows a system block diagram as an appendix.

The PDF is 14 pages: the unnumbered cover, then 13 numbered pages with one question, table or checklist per page. Short fields have a blank answer line, open-ended questions have a large blank answer box, and the checklist and yes/no questions use tick boxes.

## 1. Team and project overview

Each field has a single blank answer line.

- Team Name
- Selected Domain
- Selected SDG Track (1–17)
- Project / Solution Name
- Name (Leader)
- Email & Phone (Leader)
- University Affiliation(s)
- Number of Team Members

## 2. Executive summary & problem statement

### 2.1. The problem context

Describe the specific, real-world challenge your team is tackling. Who is affected by this problem, and why does it matter? Highlight the critical social, environmental, or economic issues involved.

**[Maximum 250 words]**

Answer space: one large blank box.

### 2.2. The proposed AI solution

Provide a high-level overview of your proposed application. How does it apply artificial intelligence to solve the problem stated above? Briefly mention the tools or technical skills gained from the SDC Phase 1 workshops (e.g., GenAI APIs, database architecture, cloud hosting) that you intend to leverage.

**[Maximum 300 words]**

Answer space: one large blank box.

## 3. Technical architecture & implementation plan

### 3.1. Conceptual tech stack

List the technologies, frameworks, APIs, and databases you plan to use to build a functional, deployed prototype. Describe (or attach as an appendix) a simple system block diagram showing how the workshop elements connect into one architecture.

Did your project utilize any official SDC sponsor-provided GenAI API or Cloud Platform? If yes, specify which one and how it is used.

Answer space: one large blank box shared by both prompts. No word limit is stated.

### 3.2. Design-thinking & deployment strategy

Explain how your team applied design-thinking methodology to ensure this solution is technically viable, user-friendly, and ready for real deployment by Phase 2.

**[Maximum 200 words]**

Answer space: one large blank box.

## 4. Preliminary environmental & SDG impact framework

To align with SDC’s focus on meaningful global sustainability, establish your project’s impact baseline before building the prototype. Use estimated data, published research, or public benchmarks where exact figures are unavailable. Provide the citation of any sources used.

The target, baseline, and KPI you provide here will be used by judges during shortlisting to assess **Innovation and Creativity** as well as **Feasibility of build**. Vague or unsupported projections may weaken your case for advancing to Phase 2.

The section continues on two pages with a two-column table headed `Metric Category` and `Your Response`. Each row's left cell holds the metric name, a prompt and an example; the `Your Response` cell is blank for your answer. The four rows, in order:

1.  **Target SDG Sub-Target(s)**: Identify the exact UN SDG sub-target your project addresses
    - e.g., Target 11.6 “Reduce the adverse per-capita environmental impact of cities, focusing on air quality and waste management.”
1.  **Current Problem Baseline**: What is the starting condition of the problem, expressed as a number?
    - e.g., “The target infrastructure currently experiences 35% energy inefficiency due to unoptimized scheduling.”
1.  **Core Measurable KPI**: The specific unit you will track
    - e.g., % reduction in power consumption, processing-latency improvement, or kg of resource waste avoided
1.  **Phase 2 Impact Target**: Your realistic, percentage-based target by the final prototype presentation
    - e.g., “20% improvement in computational resource use.”

## 5. Eligibility & readiness checklist

Tick all boxes that apply before submitting.

- [ ] All team members have completed the mandatory GenAI, Database, and Cloud Deployment upskilling workshops.
- [ ] The project concept addresses a verified real-world problem mapped directly to one or more UN SDGs.
- [ ] The proposed solution targets a functional, deployed application rather than a theoretical business case study.
- [ ] All fields in this template have been completed within the word limits stated.

## Differences from the older copy in the GitHub template

The [GitHub submission template](github-submission-template.md) ships an older copy of this template as `submission/Submission Template SDC Hackathon.pdf`: a 6-page Microsoft Word export dated 18 October 2026, where this one is a 14-page Canva export dated 19 October 2026. Every section, field, prompt, word limit, checkbox and the file-name rule are the same in both. The differences are layout only:

- The older copy has no designed cover page, logo strip, Instagram/email footer or page numbers. Its title reads "SDC HACKATHON 2026" above "Phase 1: Preliminary Project Proposal".
- Section 1 is headed "Team & Project Overview" rather than "Team and Project Overview".
- In section 4, the older copy highlights the whole judging sentence in yellow and puts the criteria in square brackets ("[Innovation and Creativity as well as Feasibility of build]"); this version drops the brackets and highlights only the two criteria names.
- The answer space for 6.1 and 6.3 is a single line in the older copy rather than a large box, and the 6.2 table has only its header row, with no blank rows.
