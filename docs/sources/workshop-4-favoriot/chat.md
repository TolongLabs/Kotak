# Workshop 4 meeting chat

What the recording of SDC Hackathon 2026 Workshop 4, Favoriot's "AIoT: The Convergence of IoT and Artificial Intelligence" with Gopinath Rao Sinniah, on 22 October 2026, preserves of the Zoom chat: nothing. The [transcript](transcript.md) covers what was said and the [slides](slides.md) what was shown.

Text recognition run on every second of the recording found no chat text, and no one reads out a chat message. Gopinath invited questions at the start (00:00:00), before the demo (00:54:32 and 00:55:07) and at the end (01:06:19), and none were asked aloud.
