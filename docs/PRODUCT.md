# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Working adults in Malaysia, in any industry, who wonder what AI means for their job, and students, graduates and people between jobs or returning to work who want to know where to start. Four kinds come to Kotak: people who want to stay strong in their current role, people who want to move to a role they already have in mind, people who want to know where they could go, and people who aren't sure yet. They come with a worry and a few minutes, often on a phone, and should leave with a plan they can start this week.

A second audience reads the anonymous national view: officers at TalentCorp or HRD Corp who want to see which rising skills Malaysian workers lack most.

## Product Purpose

Kotak tracks how AI use is changing across Malaysian jobs and predicts which skills will rise or decline over the next 12 months. A cat counsellor asks where the user is now, what they studied and what they want, suggests roles to check when they have no job or role in mind, then asks 12 can-do questions about what they have actually done. Kotak draws their skills against what their role will need, ranks the roles they fit and builds a roadmap of real, free steps with a finish date. Success is a user who reaches a sourced roadmap in about 3 minutes, trusts it enough to start the first milestone, and comes back when new data changes it.

## Positioning

Data decides, not AI guesses. Every verdict, score and ranking comes from fixed rules over dated, sourced data; the AI only explains it.

## Brand Personality

Playful, curious, encouraging. The cat makes a scary topic approachable and keeps the user moving, the way a good counsellor would. Curiosity frames the future as something to explore rather than fear. Encouragement means every result ends in a next step the user can take, never just a warning. The playfulness lives in the guide and the motion; the data itself is shown plainly and exactly.

## Anti-references

- A government portal: dense forms, stock photos, grey tables.
- A childish game: cartoon overload that undercuts the career data.
- A corporate HR tool: bland enterprise dashboards with sidebars and KPI tiles.

## Design Principles

1. Data decides, the AI explains. Every number shows its source and date, and no screen lets an AI's guess pass as a verdict.
2. One decision at a time. Each step asks for one thing and pauses, so the user focuses instead of scanning a wall of results.
3. Honest about uncertainty. Self-reported answers, estimates, and Split or Unknown verdicts are labelled plainly, never hidden.
4. Suggest, never dictate. Kotak shows options and evidence; the user decides their own future.
5. Playful guide, serious data. The cat carries the warmth; the charts and figures stay exact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast, full keyboard use, screen-reader labels on every control and chart, and a reduced-motion alternative for every animation, the cat's included.
